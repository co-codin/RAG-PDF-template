# Northwind Robotics - Product & Operations Handbook (Demo Document)

This is a fictional handbook used to demo the rag-production service. All names,
numbers and policies are invented.

## 1. Company overview

Northwind Robotics was founded in 2019 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania by Dr. Lena Okafor
and Marcus Hale. The company builds autonomous warehouse robots and employs about
340 people across offices in Pittsburgh, Rotterdam and Osaka.

## 2. Products

### 2.1 Atlas-7 picking robot

The Atlas-7 is Northwind's flagship autonomous picking robot. It has a maximum payload
of 18 kg, a top speed of 2.4 meters per second, and a battery that lasts 11 hours on a
full charge. A full recharge takes 95 minutes using the DockStation Pro. The Atlas-7
uses a lidar-plus-camera navigation stack called PathMind.

### 2.2 Carrier-3 transport robot

The Carrier-3 moves pallets of up to 600 kg between zones. Its battery lasts 8 hours and
it supports hot-swappable battery packs, so the robot never has to leave the floor to charge.

### 2.3 Pricing

The Atlas-7 is sold for 42,000 USD per unit or leased for 1,150 USD per month on a
36-month contract. The Carrier-3 costs 58,000 USD per unit. Volume discounts start at
25 units (8 percent) and increase to 15 percent at 100 units.

## 3. Warranty and support

All robots include a 24-month limited warranty covering parts and labor. Batteries are
covered for 18 months or 1,500 charge cycles, whichever comes first. Damage caused by
operating outside the specified temperature range of 0 to 40 degrees Celsius is not covered.

Support tiers:

- Standard support: business hours (9:00-18:00 local time), first response within 8 hours.
- Premium support: 24/7 coverage, first response within 1 hour, and an on-site engineer
  within 48 hours anywhere in North America or Europe. Premium support costs 12 percent
  of the hardware purchase price per year.

Critical incidents (a robot fleet fully stopped) are escalated to the on-call reliability
team, which aims to restore operations within 4 hours.

## 4. Safety

Every robot stops within 0.5 seconds when its safety-rated lidar detects a person inside
the 1.2 meter protective field. Emergency-stop buttons are located on all four sides of
the Atlas-7. Firmware updates are signed and are rolled out to 5 percent of a fleet first
(canary), then to the remaining robots after 24 hours without incidents.

## 5. Employee policies

Employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year, plus local public holidays. Up to 5
unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Remote work is allowed up to
3 days per week for roles that do not require hands-on lab access. The annual learning
budget is 2,000 USD per employee.
